Privacy policy

Last updated 22 October 2026

This policy covers this website, famok.app. In short: we collect nothing about you here, and we built the site so that nobody else does either.

Our apps will have their own privacy policy, published before any app is available.

What this website does not do

  • It sets no cookies and uses no local storage.
  • It runs no analytics, no tracking pixels and no advertising.
  • It runs no scripts at all.
  • It has no forms and no accounts.
  • It loads nothing from anyone else: every page, style and image comes from famok.app itself, with no outside fonts or embedded content. The site's security settings tell your browser to refuse anything that does not.

Hosting

The site is served by Cloudflare, our hosting provider. Like any web server, Cloudflare necessarily receives your IP address and basic details of your request, such as the page you asked for and your browser type, in order to deliver the page and protect the site from attacks. We do not use this information to identify you, and we do not combine it with anything else. Cloudflare shows us only aggregate counts, such as how many requests the site received. Cloudflare's handling of this data is described in its privacy policy.

When you contact us

If you email or call us, we receive what you choose to send: your email address or phone number, and your message. We use it only to answer you, keep it only as long as we need it to answer you, and delete it when you ask. We never sell or share it for marketing. Our email is hosted by Proton Mail, and our phone line runs on a business phone service; both process your message so that it reaches us. To have your correspondence deleted, ask us at the address below.

Children

This website collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
