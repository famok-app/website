Privacy-first software for families.

FamOK LLC is a small, independent software company based in New York. We make apps for families, built so that what belongs to a family stays with that family.

How we work

  • No ads

    FamOK's apps and this website show no ads.

  • No tracking

    No advertising identifiers or third-party trackers. This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

  • No selling data

    We don't sell personal information.

If any of this changes, we will say so on this page before it happens, and it will never apply to information you have already given us.

What we are working on

Our first app is in development. We will share more here when it is ready, along with a privacy policy for the app written in the same plain words as the one for this site.
