Privacy-first software for families.

FamOK LLC is a small, independent software company based in New York. We make apps for families, built so that what belongs to a family stays with that family.

What we hold to

  • No ads

    Not in our apps, not on this website. We earn our living from the people who choose to pay for what we make, never from advertisers.

  • No tracking

    No advertising identifiers, no third-party trackers, no profiles built about you. This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

  • No selling data

    We do not sell, rent or trade personal information. Not to advertisers, not to data brokers, not to anyone.

What we are working on

Our first app is in development. We will share more here when it is ready, along with a privacy policy for the app written in the same plain words as the one for this site.

Questions about the company, partnerships or privacy are welcome. Email support@famok.app or see the contact page for our phone number and mailing address.
